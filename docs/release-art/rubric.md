# Release art rubric

Each criterion is scored independently, 0–10. Review the rendered images, not the names of the sets. Give absolute scores for every set, a forced ranking with reasons, the three worst problems, and one composition idea. Missing evidence counts against the relevant criterion.

| Criterion | What 10 looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Icon readability at small size | At 60 px the entire fly has a strong silhouette, an identifiable head and wings, and clear separation from the check cloth and fruit. |
| Distinctiveness and appeal | A memorable, welcoming home-screen mark; the fly, blue picnic cloth and fruit make it unmistakable. No icon text. |
| Consistency across platforms | Light, dark, square, maskable and layered TV icons share a visual identity; shelf, launch and link art feel like the same world. |
| Screenshot story in three seconds | 6–8 images per device make the living fly, feeding, flight, nervous-system X-ray, fly's eyes, varied places/time/weather and shared table clear without promotional captions. |
| Composition and colour | Intentional focal hierarchy, clean silhouettes, harmonious colour, balanced negative space and purposeful framing at each aspect ratio. |
| Technical correctness | Apple-required dimensions, opaque store/iOS images, transparent foreground TV layers over an opaque back, safe focal areas and valid maskable crop. |
| Honesty | Game-rendered assets and actual game captures; no invented features, simulated neural activity, scripted choices passed off as brain behavior or fabricated online peers. |
