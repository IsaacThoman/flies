## Description

A fruit fly, a small world, and time to watch.

Set out a crumb. Blow a gentle puff. Roll a ball past its eyes. Or leave flies alone and let its world carry on as a living wallpaper.

Under the tiny body is a simulation of 166,700 spiking neurons, connected using the FlyEM male central nervous system dataset. The simulation runs on your device. Sensory inputs reach its neurons; activity in its own neural circuits becomes walking, steering, grooming and feeding.

This is a model, not a living animal. The neurons are simplified. The game supplies hunger and sleep drives; the body supplies physics and a flight reflex. A made-up visual background stands in for missing biological input. About explains these stand-ins and the model's limits.

Watch its nervous system glow with the simulation's activity. Explore its little places, change the light and weather, or share a room with other people's flies through Online. Each fly keeps its own simulation running on its own device. No account needed.

Made for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. No ads. No sound. Just a little life to look in on.

Connectome and anatomy data: FlyEM male CNS project, HHMI Janelia Research Campus and collaborators, CC BY 4.0; modified and repacked for flies. Full credits and licences are in About → Acknowledgements.

## App Review notes

No account, sign-in, demo credentials or in-app purchases are needed. Launch the app and wait for its locally bundled brain to wake. Choose Play to interact, or Watch to leave the fly as a wallpaper. Open the menu with Back on Apple TV (Escape in the web version); on a phone or tablet use the menu button. Online is an item on that menu/title screen.

In Online, browse listed rooms or choose New room, enter a name, and create one. You can leave it unlisted and share its room link/code with a second device. Optional passwords lock a room. Online needs internet and the room service to be reachable. A room holds up to eight flies. There is no text, voice or video chat; user-entered room names can be publicly listed. The room creator can remove a fly. Leaving Online returns to solo play. Review room service URL: <room service URL>; use the production service configured into the submitted build, not development localhost.

Only WeatherKit-enabled Apple TV builds offer real outside weather. Allow location when prompted and keep real weather enabled in Settings to see it; deny permission or turn it off to use the simulated forecast. WeatherKit failure does not prevent solo play. iPhone and iPad do not request location. Apple Weather's service attribution appears in About after the service responds.

About → Acknowledgements contains the offline third-party notices. Left/right moves between notices, up/down scrolls, Back returns to About. Touch has previous/next and back controls. Privacy is served as a static web page at the privacy URL above.

The connectome drives a simplified neuron simulation; body physics, the sustained-flight reflex and visual background are explicit stand-ins, not a claim to reproduce all fly biology. There is deliberately no audio. The bar environment depicts beer, cocktails and bottles; answer the age questionnaire accordingly. Do not submit as a Kids Category app.
